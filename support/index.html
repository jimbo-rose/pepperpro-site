What PepperPro is

A calculator and a logbook. It does unit conversion and dilution arithmetic on numbers you type in, and it records what you tell it.

It does not recommend a dose, a schedule, a route or a product, does not check interactions between things you take, does not diagnose anything, and cannot verify what is in any vial. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before making any decision about a substance you take.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your device. There is no server holding your records. Signing in is optional and moves none of them — see the privacy policy.

How do I get my data out?

Settings ▸ Export. JSON or CSV, everything, handed to whatever you choose.

How do I delete everything?

Settings ▸ Erase. It removes every record, every preference, the widget's snapshot and any cached prices. Deleting the app removes the same data. There is nothing held anywhere else for us to delete.

A price in the Market tab is wrong.

Open the listing and tap Price wrong?. That flags it for re-checking. Every price shows the date it was last checked, and prices move.

Does the app work without a connection?

Everything except the features that say they send something. Those are listed in Settings, each marked LEAVES DEVICE, and each says so again at the moment you use it. The calculator and the logbook need nothing.

Do I need an API key?

Only for assistant questions the arithmetic cannot answer. Questions about your own records are computed on the device and need no key.

Reporting a problem

Email support@pepperpro.app with your device model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen. If it involves a calculation, the numbers you typed in help most.
