Privacy policy

In one sentence

PepperPro keeps your records on your device, has no account and no server of its own, and sends nothing anywhere unless you switch on a feature that says it will.

Last updated 23 September 2026.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by us. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting that identifies you.

Your vials, doses, schedules, notes, photographs, lab results and diary entries are written to a database on this device and stay there. They are included in your device or iCloud backup if you have one, under Apple's terms rather than ours.

We cannot read any of it. There is no server holding a copy.

Apple Health

If you allow it, PepperPro reads weight, steps, sleep, energy and water so they can be shown beside your own records. It is read on this device.

PepperPro never writes to Apple Health, never uses health data for advertising or marketing, and never shares it with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in iOS Settings.

The features that send something

Each is off until you switch it on, and each says so again at the moment you use it. Settings lists all of them.

Calendar sync writes dose dates to a calendar account you choose — Apple, Google, or whoever you sync with.

Dictation uses iOS speech recognition. Where your device has no offline model, iOS sends the audio to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. PepperPro keeps no audio.

Photographing a meal sends that photo to an AI provider to be recognised, and returns an estimate you edit and confirm.

The assistant answers questions about your own records on this device. Anything else goes to Anthropic using an API key you supply yourself and is billed to your account; a switch controls whether your records travel with the question.

Market prices asks our price service what compounds cost; that request is anonymous and contains nothing about you. Tapping a price opens that vendor’s own site in a browser inside the app, and reporting a price as wrong sends the listing and the figure you saw.

Sharing or exporting hands a file to whatever you pick. An export is a complete copy of your records, so where it goes then matters, and it is out of the app's hands.

How the arithmetic works

Reconstitution uses the dilution identity C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ — the amount of substance before and after adding water is the same — together with unit conversion between mcg, mg, mL and syringe marks.

None of it is invented here and none of it is specific to any compound. The app applies it to the numbers you type and shows its working; it holds no dosing information for any drug and has no view on what those numbers should be.

Children

PepperPro is not directed at children and does not knowingly hold data from anyone under 13.

Deleting everything

Settings ▸ Erase removes every record, every preference, the widget's snapshot and any cached prices. Deleting the app removes the same data.

There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because there is nothing held anywhere else.

Changes, and who to ask

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the app will say so rather than quietly updating a web page.
