PepperPro

Reconstitution calculator and vial logbook, for iPhone and iPad.

What it is

You type the strength printed on the vial and the dose you want. PepperPro answers with the water to add, the marks to draw on the syringe scale you chose, and how many doses the vial holds — and it shows every step of the arithmetic that produced each figure.

It also keeps the record: what you mixed and when, how old each vial is, how many doses are left, and one timeline carrying doses, measurements, lab results, notes and photographs on a single time axis.

Your records stay on your device. There is no server of its own. Signing in is optional, every feature works without it, and it moves no record off this device.

What it will not do

It does not recommend a dose, a schedule, a route or a product, does not check interactions between things you take, does not diagnose anything, and cannot verify what is in any vial.

Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before making any decision about a substance you take.
